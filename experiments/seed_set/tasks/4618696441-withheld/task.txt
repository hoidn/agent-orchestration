fix: keep the source scope of hoisted and generated bindings at 2.33

A binding that the elaborator hoists out of a match subject or out of a let*
binding value, and that the code it is hoisted over refers to, gets a fresh
name; its own references follow it. This repairs the capture of an outer name
by a match subject's let* and by an if condition's let* (the strict boolean
normalizer binds the condition whole). Other bindings keep their names, so
programs without such a clash keep their step identities.

Generated loop-state field bindings are chosen apart from every identifier in
scope and in the continue form, and are named by the case arms that enclose
them, so two continues in different arms no longer share a name.

An effectful if or match whose value is bound in a loop body is refused with
the located diagnostic compiler_defect_loop_control_value instead of an
internal exception. Targets below 2.33 are unchanged.

A check (`dev-check.json`, run by `python dev_check.py`) runs a test that is kept out of the repository; it fails now and should pass once the change is made. Leave `dev-check.json` and the existing tests unchanged; you may add new tests.
