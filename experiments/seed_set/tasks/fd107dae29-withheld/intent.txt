fix: derive the replay index of every called workflow in --dry-run and keep a replay rejection when its source location is unavailable

--dry-run now derives the pure-result replay index of the root and of each
callee a fresh run opens under the replay profile, once each, resolved as
execute_call resolves it; a callee rejection names its call sites. The
rejection is placed from the root build's source map, scoped to the
rejected workflow; a map that cannot place it becomes a note, and the
rejection is printed at the workflow file without a line. resume renders
the rejection with the same function as run.
