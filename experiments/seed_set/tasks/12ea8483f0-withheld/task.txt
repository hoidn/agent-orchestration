fix: keep imported procedure effects in effect inference from target 2.33

A local procedure that calls an imported effectful procedure lost the callee's
effects after the first round of the effect inference fixpoint, because the
effect map was rebuilt from local procedures only. The wrapper was then
inferred effect-free and inlined as pure, and a match subject or let* binding
of its result failed after typecheck. From target 2.33 the summaries of
procedures compiled in earlier modules stay in every round, so the wrapper
must declare the imported effect, and a std/improve helper specialized with an
imported hook lists that hook's effects. Targets up to 2.32 are unchanged.

The wrapper in the std/improve test that relied on the defect now declares
its effect.

A check (`dev-check.json`, run by `python dev_check.py`) runs a test that is kept out of the repository; it fails now and should pass once the change is made. Leave `dev-check.json` and the existing tests unchanged; you may add new tests.
