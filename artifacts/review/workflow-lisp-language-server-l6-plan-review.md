## Approved execution contract

The plan preserves L6a hover, L6b reverse-L5 references, and L6c standalone
TextMate grammar as independent activation and closure units. Each unit owns
its direct TDD cycle, exact production or asset paths, focused and real
stdio/oracle evidence, ordered implementation and final reviews, broad
non-security gate, routing closure, and postcommit control. Compiler/frontend,
LSP state/compile-driver, runtime, provider, prompt, workflow, security,
safety, secrets, and provider-isolation paths remain protected.

The plan makes the pinned L6c engine boundary executable: generic presentation
is guaranteed through nesting depth 20; production-valid depths 21, 50, and
100 and malformed recognizer misses use a non-invalid ordinary-symbol
fallback; the production parser and real pinned TextMate/Oniguruma oracle own
the opposing acceptance evidence.

## Selection limit

Plan approval selects no implementation. The current `activated_units` set is
empty. Implementation may begin only after a later explicit owner decision
names a non-empty subset of `L6a`, `L6b`, and `L6c` through the plan's fixed,
non-self-referential activation fields. Activating one unit never activates
another, and no L6 capability is implemented or copy-safe from this record.
