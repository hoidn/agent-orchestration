# Documentation Role Cleanup — 2026-10-15

Status: completed and integrated into the working checkout. This is a dated execution
record, not behavioral authority. The [documentation conventions](../documentation_conventions.md)
own role policy; the [hub](../index.md), [design index](../design/README.md) and

## Scope And Decisions

The audit inventory covered 1,466 document entries, including 1,255 tracked entries,
and separately inspected discovery/selector manifests and ignored local evidence.
Focused reviews covered component designs, specs, guides, plans, backlog and
operational catalogs. The cleanup resolves the 37 observations below; it does not
rewrite every historical record or certify every runtime capability.

- `AGENTS.md` and the hub legend explicitly distinguish document role from
  implementation status and route to the shared documentation conventions.
- Specs, component contracts, architecture and runnable guides explain present
  behavior and its prerequisites. Necessary version/type/reader contracts remain.
- Indices provide current purpose, availability and owner routes. Active roadmaps
  retain selection and remaining gates; delivered chronology links to history.
- Reports, closed plans, preregistrations and frozen records may retain chronology,
  obsolete commands and historical claims when their role is explicit. Their
  contents do not independently authorize new execution or experimental spending.
- Forty backlog proposals retain their original bodies and machine frontmatter.
  The [backlog inventory](../backlog/README.md) distinguishes 12 open/unselected
  proposals, 11 preserved gates, 10 retired-only proposals and 7 historical records.
  No item was moved to `done/` or implicitly selected.

Three contracts previously buried in delivery plans now have maintained owners:
[prompt dependencies](../design/workflow_lisp_provider_prompt_dependencies.md),
[provider call policy](../design/workflow_lisp_provider_call_policy.md), and
[OMP providers/native continuation](../design/omp_provider_and_native_continuation.md).
Their original plans remain historical evidence. OMP's retained native continuation
and provider templates do not restore retired creation/import routes or select I2.
