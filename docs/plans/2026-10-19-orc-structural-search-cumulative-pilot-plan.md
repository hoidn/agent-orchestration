## Review Focus

1. A resumed campaign must continue the interrupted one, not restart it: after a kill between generations or mid-generation, logged episodes and evaluations are never repeated, the partial generation completes from the log, the same `run_seed` and scripted outcomes draw the same parents as an uninterrupted run, no trial is added by the resume, and the ingestion position advances (Task 4 `test_resume_continues_the_same_draws_and_adds_no_trial` and `test_mid_generation_kill_resumes_without_repeating_settled_work`).
2. Calls must never become the cost axis of a campaign frontier: a candidate whose receipts are incomplete stays off the frontier and is reported even when every call resolves to one (model, effort) pair (Task 2 `test_campaign_frontier_refuses_the_calls_proxy_and_keeps_unmetered_candidates_off`).
3. The brief's structural summaries must carry structure only and only this campaign's: no judged value, hidden fraction, pool or evidence path, other candidate's cost, validation/sealed outcome or sibling campaign's child reaches `EPISODE.md`, and the store root is masked from every episode and scan-detected (Task 3 `test_structural_summaries_carry_no_scores_paths_or_outcomes`, Task 4 `test_the_store_root_is_masked_from_every_episode_and_scan_detected` and `test_two_interleaved_campaigns_under_one_criterion_never_see_each_others_children`).
4. Reservoir membership must convey nothing: a reservoir member never enters the frontier, the endpoints or the final choice while it is dominated, even as the parent of a frontier entry (Task 4 `test_stepping_stone_reaches_c_only_through_the_mixture_and_b_stays_off_the_frontier`).
5. Split access must persist across campaigns without blocking the study itself: exposure is recorded on actual use, a package exposed as adaptive is refused as validation or sealed, a package exposed as validation is refused as sealed, two campaigns under one criterion both start with the same declared validation and sealed packages, validation runs once, the sealed split opens once and only after a validation-based final choice and the `frozen` disposition (Task 1 `test_a_task_exposed_in_an_earlier_campaign_is_refused_as_validation_or_sealed` and `test_two_campaigns_under_one_criterion_share_declared_roles_and_the_sealed_package_opens_once`, Task 5 `test_sealed_split_opens_once_after_validation_and_freeze_and_never_reaches_an_episode`).

## Decisions

1. **One new module, `campaign.py`; the loop lives in `adaptive.py`.** `adaptive.py` (358 lines) keeps its refusing `run_mode2` and historical helpers and gains `run_campaign` (about 140 lines). If it would exceed 500 lines, `validate_finalists` and `mode2_readout` (read-only interpreters of frozen Phase 2 evidence) move to `scoring_phase2.py` (294 lines), a split at an existing responsibility. Reason: the design's minimal footprint ([Persistent Evidence Store](../design/orc_structural_search_cumulative_campaigns.md#persistent-evidence-store)) and the module plan's size rule.
2. **Store records are six kinds** on one JSONL file, each `{"schema": "campaign-evidence-v1", "kind": ..., "seq": n, ...}`, as the design's table: `campaign` (label, `campaign_id`, `context_key`, policy and frozen parameters, budget declaration, seed set, `run_seed`, warm-start selection (the initial-evidence trial ids) with its acquisition cost, declared validation and sealed packages, roots), `subject` (`candidate_id`, parent, source manifest reference, skeleton, allocation policy set, `idea_key`, behaviour descriptor, registering campaign), `trial` (`trial_id`, `origin_trial_id | null`, `campaign_id`, `evaluation_id`, `run_id`, `state_dir`, `attempt`, `context_key`, `candidate_id`, task, replicate, split, generation, outcome, calls, wall clock, consumption or `unavailable`, `receipt_usage`, `records_dir`, incidents), `assessment` (`trial_id`, pool results path, `clone_ids`, judge totals, value, hidden, `criterion_sha256`, qualification proof hash, the split's access class), `disposition` (`target_kind` trial or campaign, `target_id`, `kind` in `contaminated | invalidated | superseded | frozen | sealed_opened`, reason, superseded records), `exposure` (package sha256, role actually used, campaign, `criterion_sha256`). Checkpoints are files, not store rows.
3. **Identities** ([Identities](../design/orc_structural_search_cumulative_campaigns.md#identities)). `context_key = "ctx1:" + sha256` over the sorted criterion-frozen tuple: adaptive package sha256s, criterion sha256 (which covers `models`, `model_policy`, `price_table`, judges, rubric, horizon and `B_plan`), runtime commit, `EVALUATOR_VERSION`, the criterion's `envelope` (episode and evaluation wall clocks, `per_call_timeout_sec`), the model policy; replicates, concurrency, horizon and budget are campaign parameters outside the key's inputs but inside the criterion hash, so pooling holds within one criterion and a follow-up study under another criterion runs its own warm start. `campaign_id = "<label>-<store sequence>"` (label `[a-z0-9-]{1,32}`; a label may not be reused in a store); the warm start is its own campaign per context, `campaign_id = "warm-" + context_key[5:17] + "-" + sequence` (the twelve digest characters). `trial_id = "trial:" + sha256(campaign_id, run_id, evaluation_id, attempt)`; `origin_trial_id` is recorded on copied or derived records and only original trials count as samples; an incident resume keeps its `evaluation_id`, `run_id` and `trial_id`; a quota relaunch is a new attempt and evaluation id under the same job (`evaluation.evaluation_id`), the superseded attempt gets a `superseded` disposition and its consumption counts.
4. **Estimates** are empirical and pool by context ([Estimates And Attribution](../design/orc_structural_search_cumulative_campaigns.md#estimates-and-attribution)): per candidate and `context_key`, across campaigns (whole-program evidence reuse), per-task primary means (`scoring.primary_value`, failure-inclusive) over non-disposed original trials, quality = equal-weight mean over the adaptive tasks with every task observed at `min_trials: 1` (`support: every_adaptive_task`), cost = mean `pricing.cost` per trial (any unavailable → unavailable); every estimate carries `estimator_version`, `context_key`, `kind: "observed"`, per-task `n`, SD over independent trials or `insufficient_support` when `n < 2`, attempt and disposed counts, excluded trials; no trials → `unobserved`, never a score. Which candidates a campaign's archive holds is decided by Decision 7a, not by the estimate.
5. **Quality floor** = the mean stub-anchor total per task over the campaign's own adaptive pools (`pareto_readout.stub_floor` over `read_pool` results), recomputed at every checkpoint; missing stub evidence → floor unavailable → no frontier that generation.
6. **Reservoir and policies.** Admission: valued, uncontaminated, non-frontier candidates of this campaign (quality numeric under the failure rule; below the floor or cost-unavailable candidates included: "neutral or worse" is the point); eviction FIFO by admission generation when over capacity 4. `frontier_only`: every draw uniform over the bounded frontier; `mixture`: draw index 0 of each generation uniform over the reservoir (empty → frontier draw, logged `reservoir_empty`), the rest uniform over the frontier. The fraction is positional, not Bernoulli, so both campaigns spend exactly the same draws. Reservoir membership is never reported as a result.
7. **Revisit key: digest, or skeleton plus policy set** (the design's rule), matched against the candidates already evaluated or scheduled in the current generation in this campaign, initial evidence included. `record_normalization.skeleton_hash` maps `:model`/`:effort` to `#lit`, so a policy-only rewrite hashes equal to its parent; it is a fresh `policy_only` child when its policy set differs from every set seen with that skeleton in this campaign, and a revisit otherwise. A prompt-only rewrite under `module` still hashes equal and is a revisit: a disclosed ceiling named in the criterion (`revisit_key`). The three-arm readout's 17 distinct skeletons among 18 children show this key would have flagged at most one: semantic convergence is not caught by revisits; the operative pressure is the brief's "already tried" list with the `idea_key`, and no mechanism guarantees semantic novelty.
   - **7a. Campaign scoping.** A campaign's candidate set is its recorded initial-evidence selection (the shared warm start's candidates) plus its own evaluated children plus its current generation's scheduled children; the match set, the brief summaries, the convergence diagnostic and the archive, frontier and reservoir rebuild (`rebuild(store, campaign_id)`) read that set only, never a sibling campaign's children, even when campaigns interleave under one criterion. Estimates for a candidate in that set still pool every trial of equal `context_key`. Selection, briefs and stop rules therefore see only the campaign, which keeps the two policy arms independent with identical initial evidence.
8. **Warm start is shared, separately costed, and is the live smoke.** The warm-start campaign (capped `direct`, the reference seed, and capped `implement_check_diagnose_repair`; 2 replicates per adaptive task) runs once per `context_key`; its first evaluation serves as the live smoke (no separate smoke step or row); both policy campaigns record the same trials as initial evidence; its acquisition cost is recorded on the `campaign` records and reported beside, never inside, a campaign's cumulative budget. `c_ref` and `q_ref` are the reference's warm-start figures.
9. **Budget is a union of receipts** keyed by (`records_dir`, `call_id`) over every episode, evaluation (superseded attempts included) and judge row of the campaign; priced cost is `unavailable` when any receipt is, calls and wall clock always; unpriced work (operator adjudications, reruns, interventions) is a listed column, never summed in.
10. **Endpoints at predeclared cuts** ([Endpoints, Budget Accounting And Power](../design/orc_structural_search_cumulative_campaigns.md#endpoints-budget-accounting-and-power)). `B_plan` is the per-campaign planned metered budget, computed at freeze from the price table and the planned calls with the per-call token figures measured in the measurement plan's Steps A–C, and recorded in the criterion; cuts B₁ = 0.5·B_plan, B₂ = 0.75·B_plan, B₃ = B_plan; the readout at a cut is the last checkpoint whose cumulative metered cost does not exceed it; per checkpoint: best frontier quality under deployed-cost ceilings {1, 2, 4} × `c_ref` and cheapest frontier cost meeting quality targets `q_ref` + {0.5, 1.0, 2.0} (rules frozen; the resolved numbers are recorded in the ledger after P0 and passed to the readout as arguments, since `selection.final_choice` needs a numeric ceiling). Cost is primary; calls cuts at 50, 75 and 100 % of the planned calls are reported as a labelled secondary proxy only when every call resolves to the two admitted models at their fixed efforts; a campaign with any unavailable receipt is "not comparable at cost cuts", never silently. Final independent assessment: the validated frontier (finalists and the reference on the validation split, `selection.final_choice` over validation rows), then the sealed-split quality of each campaign's final choice and the reference.
11. **Three splits, always.** Selection among produced candidates and the final choice use validation evidence; the sealed split is the promotion assessment, opened once after the `frozen` disposition; adaptive evidence never selects a finalist for promotion. Without a qualified validation package the pilot is descriptive: adaptive trajectories and endpoints at cuts only, no final choice, no sealed opening, and the report says so.
12. **Exposure on actual use.** An `exposure` record is written when a role is actually used: `adaptive` at campaign start for the adaptive packages, `validation` when the validation stage launches, `sealed` when `open_sealed` runs; declared-but-unused roles are not exposures. `check_split_roles` at campaign start, at validation setup and at `open_sealed` compares the criterion's declared roles with prior actual exposures: an adaptive exposure anywhere refuses validation or sealed; a validation exposure refuses sealed; a sealed exposure refuses everything (a second sealed assessment of the same frozen study is refused by `open_sealed` itself). So two campaigns under one criterion both start with the same declared validation and sealed packages.
13. **Power first, then size.** The Power And Budget section shows the affordable design cannot detect a plausible campaign-level effect; the live steps therefore stage a descriptive pilot (one campaign per policy) whose predeclared outputs are the variance estimates a powered study needs. No policy claim is preregistered.
14. **Stop rules and cadence** ([Campaign Protocol](../design/orc_structural_search_cumulative_campaigns.md#campaign-protocol)): horizon; futility; the call cap (`CallCapExceeded`); quota exhaustion (`QuotaExhausted`); the wall-clock ceiling; invalid evidence (a generation pool whose `summarize.calibration` verdict fails P1, P2, P3 or the spread: the generation's assessments get an `invalidated` disposition and the campaign stops, reporting measure-free from that point; an unadjudicated contamination at the generation barrier; a malformed ingest); a second judge incident; persistent infrastructure failure (three consecutive non-quota incidents). Every rule reads the campaign's own evidence only. Checkpoints after every ledger settlement (each evaluation unit through `launch_units`'s `gate`, each task's judging) and after every generation; resume never repeats logged work.
15. **Family qualification beyond rungs 2 and 4 needs `amends_6`.** The frozen `calibration-amends-5.json` scopes requalification to rpi and prr and selects no further rung, so rung 1 (sealed) and the validation package are qualified under an owner-accepted successor amendment `amends_6` (same k = 2, thresholds, borderline third pair and stop rules; reviewer `gpt-5.6-terra`; scope = the named additional packages with their amended hashes; runtime validity = this study's frozen commit `I`), recorded before Q1/Q2 spend. The V2 receipt takes one rule file per package (`family.rules: {package_sha256: {file, sha256}}`), so `amends_5` covers the adaptive packages and `amends_6` the others.
16. **The accepted measure is inherited as it was accepted.** C1's criterion inherits the rubric mapping of the measure the owner accepted: Step A's reference rubric, or A′'s predeclared reference-free revision when A′ passed and the owner approved it. ## Power And Budget

**Outcome and formula.** The campaign-level outcome is Q_c, the best supported quality under a fixed deployed-cost ceiling at a fixed cumulative budget, measured on the validation split and assessed on the sealed split. The policy effect is Δ = mean(Q_mixture) − mean(Q_frontier) over R replicate campaigns per policy. The design's minimal detectable difference is `delta = (z_(1-alpha/2) + z_(1-beta)) * sigma_c * sqrt(2 / R)`; solved for the replicate count at a two-sided α = 0.05 and power 0.8:

R per policy = 2 · (z₀.₉₇₅ + z₀.₈)² · sigma_c² / delta² = 15.7 · (sigma_c / delta)²,

where sigma_c (s_c below) is the SD of Q_c across replicate campaigns of one policy and delta (δ) the minimal detectable effect.

**Assumptions (from Phase 1 and the three-arm readout).**

**Minimal detectable effect: δ = 1.0 rubric point.** Rationale: two equivalence tolerances (τ_q = 0.5), so a one-point gain changes the deployment rule's outcome; it is below Phase 1's largest observed arm gaps (1.13 and 2.00) and half the P3 mutant margin (2) the calibrated judge must resolve. Required replicates at δ = 1: R = 15.7 · (0.64/1)² = 7 per policy at the optimistic bracket, 15.7 · (1.27/1)² = 26 at the pessimistic one, so 14 to 52 campaigns (14 × 77 ≈ 1,100 to 52 × 77 ≈ 4,000 campaign calls, before the shared and final stages). At δ = 2: 2 to 7 per policy (4 to 14 campaigns: 4 × 77 = 308 campaign calls, about 370 with P0, P3 and P4 at the pilot's finalist counts, up to 14 × 77 ≈ 1,100), and only the optimistic end is affordable, on an assumption (s_s ≈ 0) nothing has measured.

**Honest conclusion.** The affordable design (one campaign per policy) has no degrees of freedom for a policy test and would detect only δ ≈ s_c · √15.7 ≈ 2.5 to 5 points, implausible on this rubric. Stage C1 therefore runs as a **descriptive pilot**: R = 1 per policy, G = 4 generations, E = 4 episodes per generation, 2 adaptive tasks, 1 replicate per adaptive evaluation, 3 validation and 3 sealed replicates per finalist. Its predeclared outputs are the first estimates of s_c's components (checkpoint-to-checkpoint and across the two campaigns), the realized acceptance, revisit, futility, reservoir-draw and `idea_key` convergence rates under `module` with capped models, the per-episode and per-evaluation cost under the price table, and the live mechanics of store, checkpoint, exposure and budget union. The policy difference is reported with its measurement-only interval (±1.96 · 0.64 · √2 ≈ ±1.8 points, search variance excluded) and no claim; a null is not evidence of no effect; the horizon is not extended after seeing outcomes. A powered follow-up is sized from the pilot's s_c, as its own owner decision, and runs its own warm start under its own criterion. Common random numbers (one `run_seed` for both campaigns) and the shared warm start reduce, not remove, the between-campaign variance. Confound disclosed: the two campaigns run one after the other and hosted model revisions are unavailable, so calendar time is confounded with policy; the owner may instead approve both campaigns before P1 and interleave them generation by generation (the same per-campaign caps apply; campaign scoping keeps the arms independent), which is the plan's recommended option when both approvals exist.

## Decision Ledger

| Decision | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Descriptive pilot, R = 1 per policy | The affordable design cannot detect δ = 1; outputs are variance estimates and live mechanics; no policy claim preregistered |
| Three splits always; validation mandatory for any choice or sealed opening | Q2 is a prerequisite with its cost in the worst case; without it the pilot is descriptive, with no final choice and no sealed opening |
| Campaign scoping of the match set, brief, convergence and the archive/frontier/reservoir rebuild | The two policy arms stay independent with identical initial evidence, interleaved or not; estimates still pool by `context_key` |
| The accepted measure is inherited as accepted | C1's rubric is Step A's or the owner-approved A′'s; V2 admits either mapping; the plan writes no claim about Step A's outcome |
| Exposure on actual use; roles checked against prior actual exposures | Two campaigns under one criterion share declared roles; validation runs once; the sealed package opens once; a package's role can never be downgraded to validation or sealed |
| `amends_6` for packages beyond rungs 2 and 4; one rule file per package in V2 | The frozen `amends_5` stays byte-identical and scoped; rung 3's historical samples do not carry over (Q2 = 8–10 calls) |
| Identities per the design | `campaign_id` label plus sequence; `trial_id` over campaign, run id, evaluation id, attempt; `origin_trial_id`; a per-context warm-start campaign (`context_key[5:17]`); `context_key` from criterion-frozen context only, pooling within one criterion |
| One new module (`campaign.py`); loop in `adaptive.py` | `run_mode2` keeps refusing; historical calls-based helpers stay read-only; split at an existing responsibility only if the 500-line rule forces it |
| Metered cost only on the campaign frontier; predeclared cuts on `B_plan` | `pareto_readout.homogeneous` is never consulted by the campaign path; calls cuts are a labelled proxy; unavailable receipts make a campaign not comparable at cost cuts |
| Digest-or-skeleton+policy revisit key against this campaign's evaluated or currently scheduled children; `idea_key` in the brief | Prompt-only rewrites are revisits (disclosed ceiling); same-idea convergence is not caught by revisits, only reported; the brief is the operative pressure |
| One futility rule, the design's | Revisit, rejected, exhausted count; fresh resets; incidents ignored; `consecutive: 4` |
| Caps by the whole-batch pre-check at a 2.0 worst realized rate with a per-child planned-maximum ceiling | Caps exceed realized plans by design (campaign cap 115 against a plan of 77); a child above the ceiling is rejected, never evaluated |
| Wall-clock ceiling 30 h per campaign | Stops the campaign, reported incomplete; derived from 3.1–5.9 h per generation |
| Checkpoint after every settlement; resume never repeats logged work | Mid-generation kills complete from the log |
| Invalid evidence stops a campaign | A generation pool failing P1–P3 or the spread invalidates its assessments; no uncalibrated evidence enters a frontier |
| Assessments through `scoring.read_pool` | The harness clone-id join and the readout qualification proof on every assessment |
| No separate smoke | The warm start's first evaluation is the live smoke; no smoke row in the budget |
| Store masked like the evidence root and scan-detected | A mutator never reads another campaign's candidates or trials |
| Every live step its own approval | The caps are ceilings per step, never a pooled allocation; Q1–Q2 are shown in the owner-facing worst case |
